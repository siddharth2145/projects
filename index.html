Biological & Organic Controls

Ladybugs

Controls aphids, mites, scale insects. Release 1,500/1,000 sq ft in early morning near affected plants.

Best for: Aphids

Lacewings

Larvae prey on aphids, caterpillars, mealybugs. Plant dill or coriander to attract naturally.

Best for: Soft pests

Neem Oil

Disrupts pest life cycle. Mix 2 tsp/litre water. Spray every 7–14 days. Avoid direct sunlight.

Organic spray

Diatomaceous Earth

Mechanical pest control. Apply dry powder at base of plants and along pest pathways. Reapply after rain.

Physical barrier

Parasitic Wasps

Targets caterpillars and beetle larvae. Effective against corn borers and tomato hornworms.

Best for: Borers

Pyrethrin Spray

Fast-acting contact insecticide from chrysanthemum flowers. Breaks down quickly in sunlight.

Rapid knockdown
Scouting rule: Walk fields in a W-pattern, check 5 plants per stop × 5 stops. Record pest counts per 100 plants to calculate infestation threshold.

pH Correction Guide

Raise pH (acidic soil)

Apply agricultural lime at 1–4 tonnes/ha. Dolomite lime also adds Mg. Retest after 6 months.

Target: 6.0–7.0

Lower pH (alkaline soil)

Apply elemental sulphur at 100–500 kg/ha. Acidifying fertilisers help long-term.

Target: below 7.5

Boost Organic Matter

Add compost (5–10 t/ha), manure, or green manure crops. Reduce tillage to preserve OM.

Target: above 3%

Soil Biological Health Indicators

Earthworm count is the best field indicator of soil health — aim for 10+ per 30×30×30 cm sample.
